Shipping containers for sale and rent in Sherbrooke & Eastern Townships

Transport M-A Roy (formerly Conteneurs Roy)

Transport M-A Roy sells and rents shipping containers for Sherbrooke and the rest of the Eastern Townships. The yard is on Route 108 in Cookshire-Eaton, about 25 minutes from Sherbrooke. Size, condition, and delivery are confirmed before the price.

20-foot and 40-foot sizes

The two standard lengths, new or used, according to what is in the yard or available to order.

20'

20 foot

The short size. It fits a driveway, a shop yard, or a small job site. It holds tools, inventory, or equipment you no longer want left outside.

40'

40 foot

Twice the length, for a job site, a farm, or a business with volume. The same use as a 20 foot, with more room for pallets, equipment, and a season of stock.

40'

40 foot with side doors

The doors open along the side. You reach what you stored without emptying the container from the end. Useful when inventory goes in and out often.

40'

Refrigerated or insulated

For farms and maple operations, when a harvest or equipment should not follow the outdoor temperature. The exact setup is confirmed with the request, according to stock.

New or used

Both are offered, for sale and for rent, according to what is in the yard. The condition of the unit is confirmed with the quote. No price list is published: each request gets an offer that includes delivery.

Delivery to Sherbrooke from Cookshire-Eaton

The truck leaves the Route 108 yard in Cookshire-Eaton. Sherbrooke is about 25 minutes away. Before the date, we check access, ground, clearance, and where to set the container. The rest of the Eastern Townships works the same way. Elsewhere in Quebec, delivery is discussed with the request.

Storage, job sites, farms, and businesses

A shipping container is closed space, on site, for as long as you need it.

Storage

Tools, inventory, files, or seasonal equipment, locked and under cover, at home or on the business lot.

Job site

Material and supplies on site during the work, rented for the length of the project or bought if the space stays.

Farm

Storage for farm equipment. Refrigerated or insulated units when a harvest or maple operation needs them.

Business

Extra inventory, seasonal stock, or a reserve, without enlarging the premises.

Common questions

Do you deliver containers to Sherbrooke?

Yes. The yard is in Cookshire-Eaton, about 25 minutes from Sherbrooke. Delivery is part of the quote: we confirm the address, the access, and the placement before setting the date. We also deliver elsewhere in the Eastern Townships and, on request, across Quebec.

Can I rent a 20-foot or a 40-foot container?

Yes. Rental and sale cover the same sizes: 20 foot, 40 foot, 40 foot with side doors, and refrigerated or insulated units according to stock. The rental term follows the project. What is available is confirmed with the price request.

Are the containers new or used?

Both, according to what is in the yard. A used unit is still a steel container checked before sale or rental. The condition of the unit you choose is described with the quote.

Do you have side doors, or refrigerated and insulated models?

Yes. The 40 foot with side doors opens along its length. Refrigerated or insulated models are used mainly for farming and maple operations. Roll-up doors and other modifications are requested on the quote, starting from the container you choose. Availability changes with stock.

How do I get a price?

Use the quote form, or call 819 578-3441. Give the size, sale or rental, and the delivery municipality. There is no published price list: the offer includes the container and delivery, according to what is available.

Ask for a price for Sherbrooke or the Eastern Townships

The form is where you give the size, sale or rental, and the municipality. We reply with what is in stock and a price that includes delivery.
